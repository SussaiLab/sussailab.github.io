What SUSSAI offers

🪜 Real Ladder

NO/NC contacts, edges, coils, TON/TOF timers and CTU/CTD counters. Addresses in WEG Clic02 style (I01, Q01, M01) or LogixPro style (I:1/0, O:2/0, B3:0/0).

🏭 Machines in 3D

Conveyors, traffic lights with pedestrians and cars, mixing tank, bottle filling, parking barrier, building water tank, turntable, press and elevator – all obeying your program.

🛠 Maintenance training

Overload relay tripping with smoke, dirty sensor, broken wire, welded contact, burnt-out lamp, two-hand control bypass. The student must find and explain it.

🛑 NR-12 safety

Emergency stop button on every panel, two-hand control with simultaneity and light curtain. Colors according to IEC 60204-1.

🏆 Gamification Mode

Guided exercises: operate the machine, questions, hidden fault and fixing the program. Score from 0 to 10 saved per student.

➕ Extra scenes (.cena)

Create new machines and share them with other instructors: just add the .cena file to the program.

Frequently asked questions

In what language is the program?

SUSSAI is available in English (US), Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil). Choose the language in the 🌐 box on the top bar of the program or on the login screen – menus, help, tutorial, exercises and messages change completely. The User Manual in English uses the same names you see on screen. Only the I/O addresses and tags (I0, Q1, B1, K1…) are the same in every language.

How do I pay?

Fill in the form of the Buy button: the order e-mail is created ready to send. We reply with the payment details (Pix, bank transfer or an international option) and release the license as soon as payment is confirmed. Prices are in Brazilian reais (R$).

Do I need internet?

Only to sign in with the Google account and release the license. After that SUSSAI works offline until the end of the plan.

On how many computers can I use it?

Individual plans allow 2 computers at the same time with the same Google account. The group license allows up to 20. Changed computers? Use “Desconectar este computador” (Disconnect this computer) in Ajuda ▸ Licença (Help ▸ License).

What happens when the plan ends?

SUSSAI stops opening until it is renewed. Your programs and scores remain saved on the computer.

Windows showed a warning when opening it. Is it safe?

Yes. Because it is a new program, Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info” ▸ “Run anyway”.

Can I cancel the purchase?

Yes, within 7 days after release, with a refund (Brazilian Consumer Protection Code, art. 49). See the Terms of use.

Is there a manual?

Yes: User Manual in PDF with all the machines, inputs/outputs, exercises and troubleshooting.

How to take part

  1. Record using SussaiLab (class, challenge, scene, tip, class project...).
  2. Publish it on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn or Vimeo – use #SussaiLab and tag @sussailab.
  3. Sign in with your Google account here and send the link.
  4. Once approved, the video shows on this page and you get an e-mail. 🎉

💡 Tips for a great video

  • Show the simulator screen and explain what you are doing.
  • Short videos (1 to 5 min) get the most views.
  • Say the name of your school, course or company – we promote you too!
  • No personal data of underage students without permission.

SussaiLab Electrical Panel Assembly

Build an electrical panel in 3D: DIN rail, ducts, breakers, contactors, terminals and labeling.

Planned features

3D panel

Mounting plate, DIN rail, ducts and door – build it part by part, like on the workbench.

Real components

Breakers, contactors, overload relays, power supplies, terminals, PLC and door push buttons.

Wiring and labeling

Conductor colors, wire markers, ferrules and terminal strips – with automatic checking.

Good practices and safety

Simple conductor and protection sizing, grounding and a handover checklist.

How it will work

  1. It comes inside SussaiLab (launcher): when it launches, it shows up unlocked on the programs screen.
  2. Open it and sign in with the same Google account – no new sign-up.
  3. Use it on 1 computer at a time; signing in on another one asks the previous one to sign in again.
  4. First time on a computer: confirm the code sent to your e-mail.

This program will be free – your SussaiLab account is all you need. No launch date yet.

Electrical control CAD and simulator: draw power and control circuits, press F5 and watch contactors, timers and motors work – with 16 3D machines obeying your diagram.

What is inside

153 components

Supply, protection (fuse, breaker, RCD, motor breaker, overload relay, phase loss, surge protector), contactors, timers, impulse relay, counter, buttons, limit switches, sensors, pilot lights, motors.

44 ready diagrams

Direct-on-line, reversing, star-delta, autotransformer, Dahlander, soft starter, drive, single-phase 127/220 V and the diagram of every scene – all simulating right away.

Real simulation

Colors by potential, step by step (F9), speed from 0.01× to 4×, check circuit (F7), detect wiring fault (Shift+F7), overload relay that trips.

Professional sheets

A4 to A1 with frame, zones, title block and cross references. Print to PDF and export PNG and SVG.

Classroom tools

Motor connection school (F3), automatic wire-by-wire assembly (F4), real 2D view of the components (Shift+R).

3 languages

Português, Español and English – program and manual.

16 3D machines

Your diagram tags drive the machines: coil K1 runs motor K1 in the scene and sensor B1 in the scene operates contact B1 in the drawing. Limit switches act only when the part touches them.

How to start

  1. Download SussaiLab.zip and extract it (right-click ▸ Extract all).
  2. Open SussaiLab.exe and sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click card 02 – SussaiLab Comandos Elétricos ▸ Open.
  4. Only the 1st time on each computer: confirm your Google account. Then it opens directly, even offline.

Quick questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Just your SussaiLab account (the same Google account). No card, no time limit.

Does it program PLCs?

No – for Ladder use SussaiLab CLP, in the same SussaiLab.exe.

Can I copy it to other computers?

Yes, freely. On each new computer it asks for the Google sign-in only once; never again after that.

Why sign in to download?

It is the free SussaiLab account registration: one click with Google, no new password.

I already have SussaiLab

Download the new SussaiLab.zip and replace SussaiLab.exe: the Comandos card appears unlocked.

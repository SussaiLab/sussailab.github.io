What SUSSAI CLP offers

🪜 Real Ladder

NO/NC contacts, edges, coils, TON/TOF timers and CTU/CTD counters. Addresses in WEG Clic02 style (I01, Q01, M01) or LogixPro (I:1/0, O:2/0, B3:0/0).

🏭 Machines in 3D

Conveyors, traffic lights (with pedestrians and cars, or lamps only), mixing tank, bottling, parking gate, building water tank, turntable, press and elevator – all obeying your program.

🛠 Maintenance training

Overload relay tripping with smoke, dirty sensor, broken wire, welded contact, burnt lamp, two-hand control bypass. The student has to find and explain it.

🛑 NR-12 safety

Emergency stop on every panel, two-hand control with simultaneity and light curtain. Colors according to IEC 60204-1.

🏆 Gamification Mode

Guided exercises: do it on the machine, questions, hidden fault and fix the program. Score from 0 to 10 and a 💡 lamp that shows where the error is.

🔊 Sound and 3 languages

Motors, horn, liquids and buttons with adjustable volume. Program, manual and website in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
